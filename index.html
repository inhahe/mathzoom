Four infinite numbers

Each page starts on the end of a formula for a huge number and zooms out for ever, faster and faster, until you click — then the formula gets its outermost level and you are looking at one whole, finished number. Left to right, each way of writing it is a whole tier more powerful than the one before.

The rungs count steps and folds. A step repeats the operation before it (+, ×, ^, ↑↑, …); a fold, ω, jumps to “as many steps as the number itself”; ω² folds the folds; ε₀ is where ω, ωω, ωωω, … is heading. N is how many levels deep your finished number is — you choose it by when you click.
